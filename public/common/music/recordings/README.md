# Full performance recordings

These files are for optional listening in the song picker. Gameplay continues to use the game's separately timed, shortened arrangements; these recordings are never used as a rhythm track or as conductor-controlled accompaniment.

- `fur-elise.mp3`: Beethoven, *Für Elise* (WoO 59), piano performance by V Gao (Gaodifan). [Original recording and copyright holder's public-domain/CC0 dedication](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:FurElise.ogg), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The original Ogg Vorbis was converted to MP3 at 128 kbps; no musical cuts were made.
- `gymnopedie.mp3`: Erik Satie, *Gymnopédie No. 1*, guitar arrangement and performance by Michael Laucke. [Original recording, artist's copyright permission and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Satie_Gymnopedie_No_1_performed_by_Michael_Laucke.flac), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The original FLAC was converted to MP3 at 128 kbps; no musical cuts were made. The converted recording is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
