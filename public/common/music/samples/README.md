# Instrument sample credits

The MP3 instrument notes in this directory are adapted from [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) by Versilian Studios LLC. The original recordings are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE). See also the [creator's official release](https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/).

Selections are from Solo Violin / Arco Vib, Violin Section / susVib, Cello Section / susvib, Solo Contrabass / SusNV, Trumpet / sus, F Horn / sus, Flute / susNV, Upright Piano, and Glock. They are converted from the original WAV files to mono 44.1 kHz 96 kbps MP3 and level matched; sustaining samples have a short crossfade at the loop point. The number in each filename is the source note's MIDI number. These are recordings of individual instruments, not recordings of complete classical works. The playable pieces are the game's own shortened arrangements.
